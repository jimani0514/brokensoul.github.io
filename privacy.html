4. Broken Tactics — Android

Broken Tactics uses Supabase for authentication and supported cloud-save, player and ranking features. Its primary project is hosted in Singapore. Google Play Games sign-in, and Google account sign-in in supported builds, can provide provider IDs, player profile information and account information authorized through the provider. We do not receive your Google password.

  • Account and sessions: game user ID, provider-linked identifiers, profile/display name, email if supplied by the provider, and authentication sessions.
  • Game and ranking data: progress, save slots, character, inventory, equipment, currency, rewards, preferences, score and ranking records. Public rankings can show your display name, score and rank to other players.
  • Purchases: product identifiers, purchase state and time, quantity, entitlement and acknowledgment/consumption data. The app processes purchase tokens and platform responses to fulfill purchases. A limited verification ledger stores product and order details, an account ID and a token fingerprint to manage entitlements, prevent duplicate fulfillment and respond to support requests. After Broken Tactics account deletion, the raw purchase token is removed. Limited verification records for the deleted game profile become eligible for hourly deletion five years after deletion, unless a documented active dispute or legal hold requires them for longer. After a deleted profile is recreated, eligible purchases are restored only after fresh Google Play verification; an active profile keeps the verified records needed for that process. We do not collect full payment-card details.
  • Analytics: builds with Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics process app-instance identifiers, gameplay and feature events, device/OS/app version, coarse regional information and related usage data to understand performance and improve the game. This is separate from this website’s cookie setting.
  • Advertising: builds with AdMob support interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. Google Mobile Ads may process advertising and app identifiers, IP address and approximate location, ad/app interactions, and diagnostics. Where required, an in-game consent message and available privacy options control ad-related choices. Ads may be limited or unavailable depending on consent, region, age signals, connection and provider availability.
  • Notifications (optional): Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) delivers game news, event and mail/reward notifications when you enable them. We process your game account ID, a randomly assigned installation ID, FCM registration token, notification preference and language, notification identifiers and delivery results. You can turn notifications off in the game or block them in Android settings. Turning them off in the game disables delivery in the app; after a successful server sync, our server disables the registration and removes its stored FCM token. Google handles its own service records under its privacy policy.

Ad-removal entitlements affect the ad types described in the purchase screen; they do not automatically remove voluntarily requested rewarded ads or all analytics. This game does not request precise GPS, contacts, camera or microphone access for its described gameplay features. Firebase Authentication and Crashlytics are not part of the current Broken Tactics release configuration. Optional notifications use FCM as described above.

Account deletion: open Settings → Session → Delete Account, or use the external deletion page. Broken Tactics cloud data, profile and ranking records under our control are removed. The game’s notification preferences, server device registrations and stored FCM tokens are also deleted. Delivery outcome records may remain after their links to the account and installation IDs are removed. The shared Supabase sign-in identity and other Broken Soul game records remain. A deletion timestamp linked to that identity remains to block older sessions from restoring erased progress. Limited purchase verification records become eligible for hourly deletion five years after deletion, and administrator audit records three years after creation. A documented active dispute or legal hold can extend the relevant period, as explained on the deletion page. Local language, audio, accessibility and consent preferences may remain. Clear app storage to remove remaining local preferences.

게임마다 대상과 기능이 다릅니다. 오늘의 꽃집은 13세 이상을 대상으로 하며, 서비스와 정보 처리는 제5항에서 안내합니다. 스토어 콘텐츠 등급과 개인정보 동의 연령은 별개입니다. 적용 법령이 아동 개인정보 처리에 보호자 동의를 요구하면 그 요건은 그대로 적용됩니다. 보호자가 아동 정보의 부적절한 수집을 발견했다면 확인·삭제를 위해 연락해 주세요.
