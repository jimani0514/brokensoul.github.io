2. Broken Soul — Android original

The original mobile game uses Google account services, Firebase services, and a Supabase backend for supported online features. The information processed depends on your build and the features you use.

  • Sign-in and player records: provider and game user IDs, account email where supplied by your sign-in provider, player name, session information, save progress, inventory, equipment, settings, rewards and ranking records. These support account access, synchronization, reward delivery and ranking integrity. Public rankings may show your player name and performance.
  • Purchases: product and order identifiers, purchase state, entitlement and acknowledgment or consumption information needed to deliver purchases, restore eligible entitlements and handle disputes. Google processes payment details.
  • Ads and analytics: AdMob and Firebase may process device or app identifiers, advertising ID, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, service improvement and fraud prevention.
  • Security and notifications: Firebase App Check / Play Integrity may process app and device integrity signals. Builds with push notifications may use Firebase Cloud Messaging registration tokens. Notifications can be controlled in Android settings.
  • Coupons: redeeming a coupon submits your game user ID and code to our backend, with redemption status and technical request logs, to prevent duplicate claims and deliver the reward to the in-game mailbox.

The primary Supabase project for this game is hosted in Seoul, Republic of Korea. Provider administration, support and ancillary processing may involve other locations described below. Coupon rewards on this website currently apply to this original Android game.

4. Broken Tactics — Android

Broken Tactics uses Supabase for authentication and supported cloud-save, player and ranking features. Its primary project is hosted in Singapore. Google Play Games sign-in, and Google account sign-in in supported builds, can provide provider IDs, player profile information and account information authorized through the provider. We do not receive your Google password.

  • Account and sessions: game user ID, provider-linked identifiers, profile/display name, email if supplied by the provider, and authentication sessions.
  • Game and ranking data: progress, save slots, character, inventory, equipment, currency, rewards, preferences, score and ranking records. Public rankings can show your display name, score and rank to other players.
  • Purchases: product identifiers, purchase state and time, quantity, entitlement and acknowledgment/consumption data. The app processes purchase tokens and platform responses to fulfill purchases. Limited purchase history and a token fingerprint may be stored with account records; we use those limited records to manage entitlements, prevent duplicate fulfillment and respond to support requests. We do not collect full payment-card details.
  • Analytics: builds with Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics process app-instance identifiers, gameplay and feature events, device/OS/app version, coarse regional information and related usage data to understand performance and improve the game. This is separate from this website’s cookie setting.
  • Advertising: builds with AdMob support interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. Google Mobile Ads may process advertising and app identifiers, IP address and approximate location, ad/app interactions, and diagnostics. Where required, an in-game consent message and available privacy options control ad-related choices. Ads may be limited or unavailable depending on consent, region, age signals, connection and provider availability.

Ad-removal entitlements affect the ad types described in the purchase screen; they do not automatically remove voluntarily requested rewarded ads or all analytics. This game does not request precise GPS, contacts, camera or microphone access for its described gameplay features. Firebase Authentication, Cloud Messaging and Crashlytics are not part of the current Broken Tactics release configuration.

Account deletion: open Settings → Session → Delete Account, or use the external deletion page. Account-linked cloud data and ranking records under our control are removed. Local language, audio, accessibility and consent preferences may remain. Clear app storage to remove remaining local preferences.

Broken Tactics는 인증, 클라우드 저장, 플레이어 및 랭킹 기능에 Supabase를 사용하며 주 프로젝트는 싱가포르에 위치합니다. Google Play Games 로그인 및 이를 지원하는 버전의 Google 계정 로그인은 제공자 식별자, 플레이어 프로필과 제공자에서 허용한 계정 정보를 전달할 수 있습니다. 개발자는 Google 비밀번호를 받지 않습니다.

  • 계정·세션: 게임 사용자 ID, 인증 제공자와 연결된 식별자, 프로필·표시 이름, 제공자가 전달한 경우 이메일, 인증 세션을 처리합니다.
  • 진행·랭킹: 진행도, 저장 슬롯, 캐릭터, 인벤토리, 장비, 재화, 보상, 환경설정, 점수 및 랭킹 기록을 처리합니다. 공개 랭킹에는 표시 이름, 점수와 순위가 다른 이용자에게 표시될 수 있습니다.
  • 구매: 상품 식별자, 구매 상태·시각, 수량, 이용 권한, 승인·소비 정보를 처리합니다. 앱은 상품 지급을 위해 구매 토큰과 플랫폼 응답을 처리하며 계정 기록에는 제한된 구매 이력과 토큰 지문이 저장될 수 있습니다. 이 제한된 기록은 이용 권한 관리, 중복 지급 방지 및 문의 처리에 사용합니다. 카드 전체 정보는 수집하지 않습니다.
  • 분석: Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics가 포함된 버전은 성능 파악과 개선을 위해 앱 인스턴스 식별자, 게임·기능 이용 이벤트, 기기·OS·앱 버전, 대략적 지역 등 이용 정보를 처리합니다. 게임 분석은 이 웹사이트의 쿠키 설정과 별개입니다.
  • 광고: AdMob이 포함된 버전은 전면 광고와 이용자가 선택하는 보상형 광고를 지원합니다. Google Mobile Ads는 광고·앱 식별자, IP 주소와 대략적 위치, 광고·앱 상호작용 및 진단 정보를 처리할 수 있습니다. 필요한 경우 게임 내 동의 화면과 제공되는 개인정보 옵션에서 광고 관련 선택을 조절합니다. 동의, 지역, 연령 신호, 연결 및 공급자 상황에 따라 광고가 제한되거나 제공되지 않을 수 있습니다.
