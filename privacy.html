Broken Soul 2: Boss Rush

This section applies to the Android game Broken Soul 2: Boss Rush, package name com.brokensoul2.mygame (the "Game").

1. No account is required

The current version of the Game does not create or require a Double J Labs account. It does not use real Google account sign-in and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or Google account identifier through gameplay.

2. Data stored locally on your device

The Game stores data locally on your device to provide game functionality. This may include game progress, unlocked content, inventory, equipment, upgrades, rewards, settings, preferences, language selection, locally generated profile or authentication-like state used only by the Game, purchase entitlements and purchase history needed to restore paid content, and a non-reversible fingerprint of a Google Play purchase token used to prevent the same purchase from being granted more than once.

This local data is not uploaded to a Double J Labs account or game server. You can delete it through Android's Clear storage or Clear app data control for the Game, or by uninstalling the Game. Uninstalling may permanently remove local progress unless your device independently preserves application data through an Android backup feature.

3. Google Play Billing

The Game uses Google Play Billing to offer optional digital products. Google Play, not Double J Labs, handles your payment method and payment account information. The Game may receive and process the product ID, purchase state, purchase time, quantity, order-related purchase data, purchase token, acknowledgement state, and signed purchase information needed to deliver, restore, consume, or acknowledge the product.

Purchase information is processed to complete your transaction, grant the content you bought, restore eligible permanent purchases, prevent duplicate grants, and help resolve transaction issues. The current version processes this information locally on your device and through Google Play Billing. Double J Labs does not receive your full card number or other complete payment credentials.

4. Google Analytics 4

The Game uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) through Google's web analytics tag to understand general usage and improve the Game. GA4 loads automatically when the Game starts. The current version does not provide an in-game analytics opt-out switch.

Depending on your device and use of the Game, GA4 may process:

  • App and gameplay interaction events, including screen or page views, sessions, and event names or gameplay parameters configured by us.
  • General device and technical information, such as device category, operating system, browser or Android WebView information, screen resolution, language, and session information.
  • A Google Analytics client identifier or a similar analytics identifier used to distinguish game installations or sessions.
  • Approximate geographic information, such as country, region, or city, inferred by Google from network information.
  • Network information necessary to transmit analytics requests.

We do not intentionally include your name, email address, phone number, Google account identifier, or the contents of locally stored save data in analytics events. Google states that IP addresses may be processed to derive general geographic information but are not logged or stored in Google Analytics. The Game does not request precise GPS location for analytics.

5. How analytics data is used

We use analytics information only to:

  • Measure general usage and engagement.
  • Understand which game screens, systems, and features are used.
  • Evaluate gameplay flow and balancing.
  • Identify general usability or performance issues.
  • Maintain and improve the Game.

We do not use analytics data to provide targeted advertising.

6. Service providers and disclosure

Analytics information is transmitted to and processed by Google LLC as our analytics service provider. The Game also requests font resources from Google's font hosting services, which may receive ordinary network and device request information. Google may process information in countries other than the country in which you live and handles that information under its own terms, security measures, and privacy policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Payments Privacy Notice
  • Information about Google Analytics data collection

We do not sell personal information and do not provide Game data to data brokers or third parties for their own advertising purposes. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to a valid legal request, protect users, investigate abuse, or protect the rights and security of Double J Labs.

7. Services not used in the current version

The current version of the Game does not use:

  • AdMob or other in-game advertising services.
  • Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase Crashlytics, or other Firebase app services.
  • Supabase authentication, database storage, or server synchronization.
  • A Double J Labs online account system.
  • Push notifications.

If these practices change in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration to reflect the change.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is removed through Android application storage controls, a device reset, or uninstallation.

Local purchase entitlements, purchase history, and token fingerprints are retained on your device for as long as needed to deliver or restore paid content and prevent duplicate grants. Clearing app data or uninstalling the Game may remove this local record. Google Play may separately retain your purchase history under Google's policies, and eligible permanent products may be restored from Google Play after reinstallation.

GA4 retention is governed by the retention setting configured for our Google Analytics property and by Google's applicable retention policies. We retain analytics information only for as long as it remains reasonably necessary to measure usage, understand gameplay, and improve the Game. Aggregated reports that no longer identify a particular analytics client or game installation may be retained for longer historical comparison.

Because the Game does not use an account and does not send names or email addresses to GA4, we generally cannot identify analytics records using an email address alone. You may request deletion of analytics information by contacting doublejmsoft@gmail.com. We will use available Google Analytics deletion tools for data that we can reasonably identify. We may request limited, non-sensitive technical information needed to locate the relevant analytics record and will explain if a record cannot reasonably be linked to your request.

9. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age required by applicable law in their country. We do not knowingly collect a child's name, email address, or account information. If we learn that information associated with a child has been collected contrary to applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. A parent or guardian may contact us using the email address below.

10. Security

Analytics information is transmitted to Google using HTTPS/TLS encryption. Access to analytics reports under our control is restricted to authorized persons. Local save data is protected by the security controls provided by Android and the user's device. However, no electronic transmission, device, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. International processing

Google may process analytics information outside your country of residence. Privacy and data-protection laws in those countries may differ from those in your country. Google applies the safeguards described for its services, and Double J Labs remains responsible for using those services consistently with applicable law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game's features, data practices, service providers, or legal obligations change. The revised policy will be posted at this public URL with an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be disclosed through the Game or another appropriate notice when required by law.

13. Contact

For questions, privacy requests, or data-deletion requests, contact:
Double J Labs
doublejmsoft@gmail.com
