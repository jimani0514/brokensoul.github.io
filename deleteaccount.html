Broken Tactics — Android

Package: com.brokensoul2.mygame

Delete inside the game

  1. Sign in to the game account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings → Session → Delete Account.
  3. Read the consequences and confirm deletion.

This deletes the Broken Tactics player record, cloud saves, profile and ranking records under our control. The game’s notification preferences, server device registrations and stored FCM tokens are also deleted. Delivery outcome records may remain after their links to the account and installation IDs are removed. The shared Supabase sign-in identity and records for other Broken Soul games are not deleted. A deletion timestamp linked to that shared identity remains so an older session cannot restore erased progress. Raw Google Play purchase tokens are removed from the game's verification ledger. Limited verification details, including a token fingerprint and account ID, become eligible for hourly deletion five years after the Broken Tactics deletion; administrator audit records become eligible three years after creation. A documented active dispute or legal hold can delay deletion until that hold ends. If the game profile is recreated, eligible purchases are restored only after fresh Google Play verification; verified purchase records are kept while the profile remains active. The in-game process clears this game's account session and progress from the current device. Language, audio, accessibility and consent preferences may remain.

Request without the game

Use the deletion request form below. State the game name and provide the in-game user ID or player name, and an account email if one is linked. A Play Games display name alone may not uniquely identify an account; we may ask for minimal additional ownership evidence.

External deletion does not remotely erase every device copy. Clear the app’s Android storage or uninstall it to remove local data. Read this game’s privacy section.

Today’s Flower Shop — Android

Package: com.todayflowers.game

This game has no developer-operated account or password, but successful Google Play Games authentication is required before Android gameplay. Declining sign-in or losing valid authentication prevents gameplay until authentication succeeds. Pausing cloud synchronization does not remove this requirement. Google Play Games data, device saves, exports, ad choices/reward receipts, billing records and support correspondence are managed separately. The advertising and billing instructions below describe the Android release being prepared on October 5, 2026; older test versions may not include these features.

Delete Play Games progress and achievements

  1. Open your Play Games profile using the Google account connected to the game.
  2. Under Your data, open Delete Play Games account & data.
  3. Under individual game data, select Today’s Flower Shop / Today Flowers and delete its data. The package above identifies this game.

Deleting the whole Play Games profile affects other games too. Review Google's confirmation and Google Play Games data deletion guide before proceeding. These controls concern Google-held Play Games data, not all records held independently by the developer or Google.

Remove device saves and exported copies

Pause the game's cloud synchronization before deletion if the in-game setting is accessible. If you cannot sign in, you can still use Google's data controls, clear app storage in Android settings and contact us about privacy rights without entering gameplay. Stopping sync or signing out alone does not delete cloud data. Clear this app's storage in Android settings to remove device progress, then delete any exported JSON files and provider backups separately. Avoid restoring or uploading an old save after cloud deletion. Local progress cannot be recovered by Double J Labs.

Ad choices, reward receipts and billing records

In Settings → Ads and privacy, change your age range or open the available Change ad privacy choices screen. Follow any restart instruction. Changing a preference affects subsequent ad handling; it does not itself erase previously processed Google data or local reward receipts.

To remove the app's saved age choice, consent preferences, frequency records, native reward receipts and local billing cache, clear Settings → Apps → Today’s Flower Shop → Storage → Clear storage/data in Android. Menu names vary by device. This also removes local game progress. Deleting Play Games data or switching the local profile does not clear these separate device records.

Ad-earned dotted/floral patterns are scoped to the original device/profile/artwork, and their native receipts are excluded from cloud saves and exported game saves. Restoration after clearing data, reinstalling or changing device/profile is not guaranteed. Paid permanent products are different: eligible ownership can be rechecked through the purchasing Google Play account. Clearing the billing cache does not cancel/refund a purchase or erase Google's transaction history.

Google-held ad, consent, diagnostic and transaction records follow Google's own privacy choices and retention rules; deleting this app is not a request to erase every Google record. Use Google's privacy controls for information under Google's control. Double J Labs cannot promise deletion of independently held platform records.

Request deletion of support records

Use the Today’s Flower Shop support form or email doublejlabs@gmail.com. State the game name, the email used for your inquiry and which records you want deleted. A player ID is only needed if relevant; never send your password or a raw purchase token. You do not need to reinstall the game or create a developer account to make this request.

We can delete information held by Double J Labs, subject to the limited retention below, but cannot delete your Google account or Google-held transaction records on your behalf. Google purchase/download records and other platform history have separate controls and retention. See the game privacy section.

What deletion means

Deletion is permanent. Progress and consumed items may not be recoverable. Account deletion does not automatically refund purchases or cancel platform subscriptions. Never send passwords, access tokens, raw purchase tokens or full card details.
  • Timing: privacy-rights requests are handled without undue delay and within the deadline applicable to the request and jurisdiction. Any necessary verification, lawful extension or limited refusal is handled with the required notices. Ordinary game-support response limits do not extend these deadlines.
  • Limited retained data: records needed for consumer complaints or proof of request handling may be kept for up to three years; legally required contract/payment records for five years and advertising records for six months where applicable. Specific active disputes or legal holds can require longer preservation. This does not authorize indefinite retention of the game account.
  • Provider data: independently held Google and Valve payment records follow their own policies. Data we cannot reasonably connect to your request and irreversibly aggregated analytics may remain. Temporary backups expire under the relevant provider’s backup cycle.
  • Other rights: you can request access, correction or deletion of particular developer-held personal data without requesting full account deletion. If removing essential data would disable a feature, we explain that effect.

For details, see the Privacy Policy. Use the deletion request form. Google sign-in may be required. If you cannot access the form, use the privacy-rights alternative contact; you do not need to create a Google account solely to exercise a statutory right.

삭제 범위와 처리 기준

삭제는 영구적입니다. 진행도나 소비한 상품은 복구되지 않을 수 있습니다. 계정 삭제만으로 환불되거나 플랫폼 정기 결제가 해지되지는 않습니다. 비밀번호, 인증·원본 구매 토큰, 카드 전체 정보는 보내지 마세요.
  • 처리 기간: 개인정보 권리 요청은 부당한 지체 없이 해당 요청·관할 법령의 처리 기한 내 대응합니다. 필요한 본인 확인, 적법한 연장 또는 일부 거절이 있다면 법정 안내 사항을 알립니다. 일반 게임 문의의 대응 제한은 이 기한을 연장하지 않습니다.
