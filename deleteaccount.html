Broken Soul 2: Boss Rush

Android package: com.brokensoul2.mygame

Delete from inside the game

  1. Open the Game and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings > Session > Delete Account.
  3. Review the warning and confirm the permanent deletion.

When the in-game process completes, the Supabase authentication account, associated cloud save and player profile, and ranking records are deleted. Account-related authentication, game-progress, and purchase-entitlement state stored by the Game on that device is also removed.

Request deletion without the game

Use the request form below or email us. Identify Broken Soul 2: Boss Rush and provide the Google account email, in-game player name, or user ID needed to locate the account. We may contact you to verify ownership before deletion.

Open deletion request form Email Double J Labs

Data deleted and data that may remain

  • Deleted: the Supabase authentication account and account identifiers, cloud-save/player data, ranking records, and account-linked purchase-entitlement and limited purchase-history data under our control.
  • Not deleted by this request: Google Play payment and order records controlled independently by Google, and GA4 records that cannot reasonably be linked to the account request. Aggregated or de-identified analytics may remain.
  • Device-only preferences: language, audio, performance, accessibility, consent, and similar non-account settings may remain. Clear the Game's Android storage or uninstall it to remove local data from the device after an external request.
  • Limited retention: deletion-request correspondence may be retained for up to three years. Records required for legal, accounting, dispute, chargeback, security, or fraud-prevention purposes may be retained for up to five years. Temporary provider backups may remain until routine backup rotation removes them.
